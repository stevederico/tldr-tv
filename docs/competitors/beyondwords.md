# BeyondWords

**URL:** https://beyondwords.io  
**Category:** Publisher audio CMS (article → on-site player)  
**Last researched:** 2026-07-13

## One-liner

AI audio platform for **publishers**: auto-generate narrated versions of articles, embed a player on the site, control pronunciation, price by article/update not runaway character regen.

## Who it’s for

- Newsrooms, magazines, content sites  
- CMS-integrated workflows (WordPress plugin historically “SpeechKit”, etc.)  
- Brands that want “listen to this article” **on their domain**  

B2B, not consumer extension.

## How it works

1. Connect CMS / API  
2. On publish (or batch), generate TTS audio for article HTML  
3. Embed BeyondWords player; stream same asset in apps  
4. Edit pronunciations; smart re-gen only for changed paragraphs  
5. Analytics on listen engagement  

## Features (relevant)

| Feature | Notes |
|---|---|
| Audio articles at scale | Core |
| Article-first pricing | Pay per article volume; cheap updates |
| Pronunciation / NLP control | Publisher quality bar |
| Embeddable player | On-site listen UX |
| Voice cloning options | Brand voice |
| 700+ voices / many languages (marketing) | Catalog depth |
| Analytics | Listen metrics for editorial |

## Pricing

- Free trial / limited free license historically (small character caps)  
- Paid: contact sales / volume — third-party complaints about steep jumps  
- Model: **predictable article-based**, not pure character panic  

Not a transparent self-serve consumer price like Speechify.

## Strengths

- Correct **publisher** packaging (embed + CMS + updates)  
- On-site “listen” without sending users to another app  
- Production controls (names, numbers, house style)  
- Aligns with “audio for every article” industry trend  

## Weaknesses / gaps vs us

- B2B sales motion, not open-web any-page  
- Audio player on publisher site — **no** chapter images / video essay  
- Reader doesn’t get a personal library of third-party web  
- We capture **reader-initiated** watch; they capture **publisher-initiated** audio  

## Overlap with us

| Dimension | Overlap |
|---|---|
| Article → narration | High |
| On-page listen | Medium (their embed vs our extension) |
| Visual watch | **None** |
| Word-sync transcript product | Low (player, not Descript-style) |

## Implications for us

1. Possible **future customer**: publishers who want video-essay embeds (BeyondWords for audio; us for watch)  
2. Near-term: not the same buyer as extension users  
3. If major news sites ship listen players, users may not need us for *those* domains — still need us for the long tail  
4. Study their embed UX for optional “publish this guide” embeds later
