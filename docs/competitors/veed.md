# VEED

**URL:** https://www.veed.io  
**Category:** Browser video editor + AI suite (subtitles, avatars, gen)  
**Last researched:** 2026-07-13

## One-liner

Popular online video editor with AI add-ons: auto subtitles, text-to-video, avatars, cleanup — “edit in the browser” for social and business video.

## Who it’s for

- Creators who want CapCut-like tools in a browser  
- Teams adding captions and quick cuts  
- Light AI generation, heavy **editing**  

## How it works

1. Upload footage or start from AI generate / stock  
2. Timeline edit in browser  
3. Auto subtitles, translate, eye contact, avatars, etc.  
4. Export by plan (resolution, watermark, minutes)  

Text-to-video exists but VEED’s moat is **editor + captions**, not article fidelity.

## Features (relevant)

| Feature | Notes |
|---|---|
| Auto subtitles / translate | Best-known feature |
| Online timeline editor | Core |
| AI avatars / text-to-video | Competitive add-ons |
| Screen recording | Built-in |
| Team workspaces | Paid |

## Pricing (order of magnitude)

Free (watermark/limits) → ~$12–$30/mo personal → business tiers. AI minutes gated.

## Strengths

- Huge SEO/brand for “online video editor”  
- Excellent subtitle UX  
- Good enough AI gen without leaving editor  

## Weaknesses / gaps vs us

- No open-web reading extension as core  
- Editor complexity vs one-tap watch
