# Descript

**URL:** https://www.descript.com  
**Category:** Transcript-native audio/video editor  
**Last researched:** 2026-07-13

## One-liner

Edit audio and video **by editing text** — record/import, auto-transcribe, cut by deleting words; AI voices (Overdub/Regenerate), clips, podcasts, screen recording.

## Who it’s for

- Podcasters, YouTubers, course creators  
- Teams that already have **recorded** media  
- Anyone who wants transcript-first editing  

Not “turn any URL into a product.” It’s a **studio**.

## How it works

1. Import or record audio/video (or Rooms multi-track)  
2. AI produces time-aligned **transcript**  
3. Edit media by editing the transcript (delete word = delete audio)  
4. Overdub / Regenerate / stock AI voices for fixes or full TTS  
5. Export video/audio; generate short clips / audiograms  

**Underlord** and other AI agents assist editing workflows (2025–26 product surface).

## Strengths

- Overdub = “fix speech by typing” (adjacent to full TTS guides)

## Weaknesses / gaps vs us

- Starts from **recording or script project**, not open-web article extract  
- No “TLDR this article” consumer path  
- Heavy editor UX — not a one-tap listen/watch player  
- You build the media; we **generate** the media from text  
- Pricing/complexity for casual article watchers is wrong fit
