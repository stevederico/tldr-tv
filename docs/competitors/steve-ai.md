# Steve AI

**URL:** https://www.steve.ai  
**Category:** Marketer script/URL → animated or live-style video  
**Last researched:** 2026-07-13

## One-liner

Patented-style automated video maker: text, prompt, blog URL, PDF, audio, or image → animated characters, live stock, or gen-AI clips for social and explainers.

## Who it’s for

- Budget YouTube/social automation  
- Marketers wanting animated explainers without After Effects  
- Same buyer as Lumen5/Pictory, often cheaper-feeling  

Claims multi-million creators (marketing).

## How it works

1. Input script, blog URL, prompt, PDF, etc.  
2. Choose style: animated, live, generative  
3. AI builds scenes with characters/stock  
4. Scene editor; export (watermark on free)  
5. Credits for AI video minutes / images / gen seconds
