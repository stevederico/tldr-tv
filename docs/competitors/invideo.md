# InVideo (invideo AI)

**URL:** https://invideo.io  
**Category:** Creator / marketer AI video; increasingly **model marketplace**  
**Last researched:** 2026-07-13

## One-liner

Prompt- and script-first AI video suite: generate finished videos with voiceovers, stock, and access to many third-party gen models (Veo, Kling, Seedance, etc.) on a credit system.

## Who it’s for

- Social creators and agencies  
- Teams that want **one UI** over many gen-video models  
- Prompt-to-video more than faithful document playback  

Marketing logos claim large enterprise brands (treat as marketing, not verified integration).

## How it works

1. Describe video (prompt) or paste script / use URL-style workflows  
2. AI generates script (often), scenes, voice, media  
3. Optional generative models consume **credits** at model-specific rates  
4. Edit in product, export without watermark on paid plans  
5. Credits reset monthly; unused do not roll over (per their FAQ)

Evolved from template video editor → **AI video + multi-model credit wallet**.

## Pricing (from invideo.io/pricing + secondary reviews, 2026-07)

Pricing is **credit-heavy** and changes often. Snapshot:

| Plan | Ballpark (annual-ish) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Limited weekly minutes, watermark |
| Plus | ~$17–$28/mo | Explore; limited credits |
| Max | ~$85/mo | Higher credits |
| Higher / Elite | ~$170–$900/mo | Power / all models / storage |

Review sources also cite older Plus/Max/Generative ladders (~$25 / $48 / $96). **Always re-check** https://invideo.io/pricing/ — model access is the product now.

## Strengths

- Breadth of **gen-video models** in one subscription  
- Strong “make a video from nothing” creator UX  
- Aggressive marketing + feature velocity  
- Multi-language / voice features in reviews

## Weaknesses / gaps vs us

- **Not about fidelity** — scripts are often AI-rewritten  
- Output quality varies by model; “generic AI video” complaints common  
- Credit complexity confuses buyers  
- Competes with Runway/Kling direct, not with long-form reading  
- No word-synced full article player  

## Overlap with us

| Dimension | Overlap |
|---|---|
| Text in → video out | Category only |
| Article faithfulness | **Very low** |
| Word sync / transcript player | **None** |
| Gen-image beats on a timeline | Conceptual cousin of our beat images (they do motion; we do illustrated audio) |

## Implications for us

- InVideo sets the **expectation bar for “AI video”** in consumer minds (flashy, short, generative)  
- We should **not** position as “another InVideo” — we lose on model breadth  
- If we export MP4, compare to **illustrated audiobook / essay film**, not Sora-class clips  
- Their credit model is a warning: opaque metering frustrates users — keep our pricing simple
