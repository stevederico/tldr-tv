# Fliki

**URL:** https://fliki.ai  
**Category:** Marketer / creator text → video; TTS-first  
**Last researched:** 2026-07-13

## One-liner

Text-to-video and text-to-speech tool: turn scripts, blog posts, and ideas into narrated videos with a large multilingual AI voice library (and optional avatars).

## Who it’s for

- Creators needing **fast voiceover + simple scenes**  
- Multilingual / dubbing-oriented workflows  
- Blog and PPT repurposing without a full NLE  

Claims: 80+ languages, 2000+ voices, 50k+ companies (marketing site).

## How it works

1. Choose mode: script, **blog URL**, PPT, idea, etc.  
2. AI pulls or generates script; splits into scenes  
3. Apply AI voice (or clone on paid) + stock/gen visuals  
4. Optional avatars, thumbnails, translation/dubbing  
5. Export video (resolution/length limited by plan)  

Blog-to-video is a first-class feature: fetch URL → summarize/scene → narrate.

## Pricing (from fliki.ai/pricing, 2026-07)

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly (effective) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | Limited credits; watermark; ~720p; short caps |
| Standard | $28/mo | ~$21/mo (25% off) | 1080p; longer exports; more voices |
| Premium | $88/mo | ~$66/mo | Longer videos (reviews cite ~40 min cap), clones, team-ish features |

Secondary reviews: free ≈ few credits/mo; Standard adds commercial rights / better music licensing; Premium for power users.

## Strengths

- **Voice quality / language coverage** is the differentiator  
- Clear blog → video and blog → audio paths  
- Free tier for trial  
- Good fit for podcasts + simple talking-head-less explainer videos  

## Weaknesses / gaps vs us

- Scene + stock aesthetic (same class as Lumen5/Pictory)  
- Blog mode is **repurpose/summarize**, not full word-synced text  
- Video length caps on lower plans  
- Creator/marketer buyer, not “watch this article” consumer  
- No deep chapter/visual essay structure

## Implications for us

- Fliki is the bar for **TTS convenience and languages**  
- If our narration sounds worse, users bounce to Fliki-class tools  
- Their blog→audio path is a **listen-only** competitor; our edge is **visual + sync + fidelity**  
- Watch their voice cloning: users will expect “read this in my voice”
