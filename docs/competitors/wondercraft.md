# Wondercraft

**URL:** https://www.wondercraft.ai  
**Category:** AI podcast / audio show from docs, URLs, scripts (+ video)  
**Last researched:** 2026-07-13

## One-liner

“Canva for AI audio”: turn docs, notes, URLs, and scripts into produced podcasts (music, multi-voice), with paths to **video** and audiograms.

## Who it’s for

- Internal comms (policies → podcasts)  
- Creators who want podcasts without mics  
- Teams translating audio into multiple languages  

## How it works

1. Start from topic, script, URL, or uploaded notes/docs (capability varies by review date)  
2. AI writes or adapts script; assign voices  
3. Generate audio with music/SFX; edit on timeline  
4. Optional: convert podcast → video; social clips  
5. Host/share episode pages  

Credit system: ~1 credit ≈ 1 minute of audio (common reporting).

## Strengths

- Strong **document → spoken show** story (enterprise internal too)  
- Production values (music, multi-host)  
- Video upsell from audio project  
- Free trial without card  

## Weaknesses / gaps vs us

- Podcast **about** content, often rewritten — not word-synced full article  
- Credit metering complexity  
- Weaker as open-web consumer extension  
- Visuals secondary (audiogram/video from podcast), not beat-synced essay images  

## Overlap with us

| Dimension | Overlap |
|---|---|
| Long text → media | High |
| Narrated essays/policies | Medium–high |
| Full transcript fidelity | Low–medium |
| Watch-with-pictures player | Low |

## Implications for us

- Competes for “I have a long doc, make it consumable audio”  
- Enterprise internal podcasts ≠ our reader wedge — but talent/budget can blur  
- If we add multi-voice “discussion mode,” we’re copying NotebookLM/Wondercraft; only if users ask
