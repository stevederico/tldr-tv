# Typeframes

**URL:** https://www.typeframes.com (also surfaced via Revid / related brands)  
**Category:** Text / site → motion graphic product-style video  
**Last researched:** 2026-07-13

## One-liner

Fast AI video tool that turns text or website content into short **kinetic / product-marketing videos** (text-on-screen, beat-synced, styled templates).

## Who it’s for

- Founders and marketers making product demos / ads  
- Social clips that look designed, not stock B-roll slideshows  
- “Text-only → polished short video” workflows  

## How it works

1. Paste text, prompt, or website  
2. AI builds scene sequence with typography, motion, stock/gen media  
3. Style presets; optional music beat sync  
4. Edit lightly; export short social video  

Often grouped with Revid-style short-form generators.

## Strengths

- Aesthetic closer to modern **product ads** than Lumen5 stock  
- Fast iteration for marketing  
- Text-as-input is simple  

## Weaknesses / gaps vs us

- Short marketing length, not long essays/books  
- Not full-text fidelity or word-timed reading  
- No browser “watch this article” for arbitrary news/essays  
- Output is ad/demo, not a reading experience  

## Overlap with us

| Dimension | Overlap |
|---|---|

| Article faithfulness | Low |

## Implications for us

- Only relevant if we chase **short social export** from a guide  
- Don’t copy kinetic-ad aesthetic for essay reading  
- One-line in landscape map is enough unless social export becomes P0
