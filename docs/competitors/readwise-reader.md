# Readwise Reader

**URL:** https://readwise.io/read  
**Docs:** https://docs.readwise.io/reader  
**Category:** Read-later library + long-form TTS  
**Last researched:** 2026-07-13

## One-liner

Power-user reading app: save articles, newsletters, PDFs, EPUBs, RSS, tweets into one library; highlight deeply; **Listen** with high-quality TTS (Unreal Speech / premium voices).

## Who it’s for

- Knowledge workers who annotate and resurface highlights  
- People who replaced Pocket (Pocket shut down Jul 2025)  
- Long-form readers who also want audio on the same library  

Not marketers. Not casual one-off page listeners (Speechify wins those).

## How it works

1. Save via browser extension, email, RSS, or URL  
2. Read in focused Reader UI (or long-form / EPUB modes)  
3. Highlight → syncs to Readwise daily review / exports (Obsidian, etc.)  
4. **Listen** button → TTS of the document (web: top-right; EPUB long-form: overflow menu)  
5. Ghostreader AI for active reading assistance  

Audio Reviews: podcast-like playback of **your** daily highlight review (not full articles).

## Strengths

- Best-in-class **library + annotation** for serious readers  
- TTS on *your* saved long-form (fidelity to full text)  
- Extension save habit post-Pocket  
- Cheap relative to Speechify Premium  
- Trust with PKM crowd (Obsidian exports)

## Weaknesses / gaps vs us

- **Read/listen**, not watch — no chapter images / video export  
- Product velocity concerns in community (some feel feature freeze)  
- Not one-tap “Watch this article” for strangers; it’s “save then maybe listen”  
- No word-timing media pipeline (generated audio essay with beats)  
- Ghostreader ≠ illustrated storytelling  

## Overlap with us

| Dimension | Overlap |

## Implications for us

1. Compete for the same **serious reader** who wants audio of full pieces  
2. Our library (`/app/home`) should feel as intentional as Reader’s inbox  
3. Differentiation = **watch mode** (visuals + captions + eventual MP4)  
4. Don’t try to beat highlight→Obsidian; partner or ignore  
5. Pricing anchor: ~$10/mo is “reader utility” price point
