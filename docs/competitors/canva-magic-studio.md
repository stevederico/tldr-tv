# Canva Magic Studio (text / doc → video)

**URL:** https://www.canva.com/magic  
**Category:** Mass-market design suite with AI video from text  
**Last researched:** 2026-07-13

## One-liner

Canva’s AI layer (Magic Studio / Magic Media / Magic Write, etc.) lets normies turn prompts, text, and designs into social videos inside the world’s default design tool.

## Who it’s for

- Non-editors who already live in Canva  
- Schools, SMBs, social managers  
- “Good enough” marketing video at freemium scale  

## How it works

1. Open Canva → Magic / video generation surfaces  
2. Prompt or paste content; pick template/style  
3. AI assembles scenes, stock, text, voice options (feature set evolves)  
4. Edit on Canva canvas; resize for platforms  
5. Export/download under Free / Pro / Teams entitlements  

Exact “blog URL → video” depth is weaker than Pictory; strength is **distribution** (hundreds of millions of users).

| Feature | Notes |

## Pricing

| Plan | Notes |
|---|---|
| Free | Limited AI credits / watermarks / storage |
| Pro | ~$15/user/mo class (region-dependent) |
| Teams / Enterprise | Seats + brand controls |

AI generation is **credit- or quota-gated** and changes often.

## Strengths

- Default tool for non-technical creators  
- Zero learning curve relative to Lumen5/InVideo for many  
- Template + resize for every network  
- Will always be “good enough” for casual blog-to-social  

## Weaknesses / gaps vs us

- Not full-article word-synced reading  
- Not open-web extension for arbitrary articles  
- Output quality ceiling for long-form essays is low  
- AI features generic, not “watch literature”  

## Overlap with us

| Dimension | Overlap |
|---|---|
| Text → video for masses | High distribution threat for **short** form |
| Long-form fidelity watch | **None** |
| Serious reading UX | None |

## Implications for us

1. Ignore for core product positioning  
2. If a user wants a 30s Instagram cut of their guide, Canva/Pictory already win — optional export later  
3. Never compete on “templates and brand kit”
