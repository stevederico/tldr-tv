# ElevenLabs (Reader + Studio + API)

**URL:** https://elevenlabs.io  
**Category:** Voice quality infrastructure + Reader app + creative Studio  
**Last researched:** 2026-07-13

## One-liner

Industry-leading AI speech company: API/voices power half the internet’s TTS; **Reader** listens to articles/docs; **Studio** builds long-form audio projects (audiobooks, narration).

## Who it’s for

- Developers embedding voices (API)  
- Creators producing narrated content (Studio)  
- End users who want best-in-class listen (Reader)  
- Enterprises for dubbing, agents, IVR  

We may **use** them as a supplier *and* compete with Reader for “listen to text.”

## How it works

### Reader
- Paste or import text / articles  
- High-quality multi-voice playback  
- Personal listening library  

### Studio
- Project timeline for long-form narration  
- Multi-speaker, sound design, long documents  
- Export audio for distribution  

### API / agents
- TTS, STS, voice clone, dubbing  
- Powers third parties (including tools like Fliki/Pictory tiers)

## Strengths

- **Voice quality** everyone benchmarks against  
- Studio is serious for full books/essays (audio)  
- API means our competitors can match our narration quality overnight  
- Brand = “sounds human”

## Weaknesses / gaps vs us

- Reader/Studio are **audio**, not illustrated video essays  
- No open-web “TLDR this page” with chapter images  
- No word-synced visual player as core product (API can power it)  
- DIY: user still needs pipeline for images, chapters, hosting  

## Overlap with us

| Dimension | Overlap |
|---|---|
| Full-text narration quality | **Infrastructure + competitor** |
| Long-form essays/books | High (Studio) |
| Consumer listen app | Medium (Reader) |
| Visual / watch / export video | **Low** |
| Word timings | They generate audio; alignment is on us (or forced aligners) |

## Implications for us

1. Users will say “why doesn’t it sound like ElevenLabs?” — plan for premium voice tier  
2. Prefer **buy vs rebuild** TTS; own sync, chapters, images, player, extension  
3. If we use another TTS (e.g. Kokoro/local), be honest about quality tradeoff  
4. Studio proves demand for **long-form narrated text** — our visual layer is the delta
